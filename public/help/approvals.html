Approving requests

Approve or deny channel requests from the approval channel.

Who can approve

System Admins can always approve. If the admin enabled Team Admins can approve requests, Team Admins of the approval team can too.

Acting on a request

Each request is posted in the configured approval channel with Approve and Deny buttons.

ActionWhat happens
ApproveCreates the channel, adds the requested members, promotes the channel admins, and posts a welcome message that @-mentions everyone added.
DenyCloses the request. The requester is notified by direct message.
Auto-approved requests

Requests from System Admins and from users on the auto-approve list skip this step — the channel is created immediately, with no Approve/Deny card.

Audit trail

If an audit channel is configured, every approve and deny posts an audit line there so there's a durable record of who acted on what.

Two-step approval: webhooks & bot tokens

Incoming-webhook and bot-token requests need two approvals — one from a security approver and one from a system approver — and they must be two different people. Eligibility comes from User Attributes your admin configures (System Admins can always fill the system step).

  • The card has a single Approve button (shared by everyone who can act); the Approvals section tracks each step's status — who has signed off and which step is still outstanding.
  • The first approval keeps the request open and updates the card; the second, from a different qualified person, completes it.
  • On completion the webhook URL / bot token is DM'd privately to the requester — it is never posted in the approval channel.
  • Either approver can Deny to reject the whole request.
