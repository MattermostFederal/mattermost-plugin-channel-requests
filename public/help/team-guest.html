Docs / Guest invites

Request guest invite

Request a guest invite for a team. Guest email is required. On approve, Mattermost emails a channel-scoped guest invitation (Guest Accounts must be enabled). A regular team signup link is never returned.

Tickets go to mattermost-team-requests. Slash: /mattermost-request-hub team-guest.
